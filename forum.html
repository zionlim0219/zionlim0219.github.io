hall of vib (very important brats)

a nostalgic archive of celebrities and pop culture moments tied to the intersection of fame, nightlife, and excess
britney spears
a pop machine built too young and too fast. her breakdown wasn’t just personal, it was public property. a martyr and symbol of what happens when fame eats its own.
amy winehouse
a once-in-a-generation voice caught between raw talent and self-destruction. despite her stardom, with her drug abuse brushed aside, the world couldn’t look away until it was too late.
lindsay lohan
the face of early-2000s excess. child star, club regular, tabloid headline, her story blurs the line between performance and survival.
paris hilton
a socialite who turned scandal into empire #thatshot. she invented influencer culture before we had words for it, selling a fantasy of fame, nightlife, and oblivion.
demi lovato
famous for disney and struggling with drug abuse and addiction, facing relapses and hospitalizations, and uses her experiences to raise awareness about mental health and recovery.
mac miller
a modern voice for vulnerability, gone too soon from his accidental overdose - his music and story live on through his legacy.
jimi hendrix
legendary guitarist whose groundbreaking music and drug-fueled lifestyle made him a rock icon, gone too soon at 27.
miley cyrus
experimented with drugs and self-destructive behavior but has been open about growth and recovery.
whitney houston
the ultimate star with a voice unmatched, a story shaped by fame’s extremes.
justin bieber
global superstar who has admitted to partying and substance struggles, now focused on sobriety.
kurt cobain
the iconic frontman of nirvana who turned pain, addiction, and fame’s pressures into raw, timeless music, tragically dying young.
